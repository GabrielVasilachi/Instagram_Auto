# Video Editor

After signing in, select **Video Editor** from the menu or open `/video-editor` on your site.

1. Click **Import media** and choose videos, images, or audio files from your computer.
2. Drag files onto the timeline. You can move, trim, and split clips, add text, and preview the result.
3. Click **Export**, then **Export video** to create an MP4. Click **Download video** to save it to your computer.
4. To post it on Instagram, choose **Send to Instagram Planner**, enter a caption and time, and confirm the schedule. Exporting alone **does not publish** anything. The project must be vertical (9:16), at least three seconds long, and have Cloudinary configured.

Your project and original files stay in this browser on this device. Clearing the site's browser data removes them, so save the exported MP4 separately. For installation and Instagram setup, start with [README.md](README.md).
